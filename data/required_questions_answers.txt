- IT and Business Services: 21,803 (Q3 FY25), 21,544 (Q2 FY25), 20,417 (Q1 FY25)

- HCL Software: 3,387 (Q3 FY25), 2,865 (Q2 FY25), 3,422 (Q1 FY25)

To calculate the growth rate, we can use the formula: ((Current Quarter Revenue - Previous Quarter Revenue) / Previous Quarter Revenue) * 100

For IT and Business Services:
- Q3 FY25 to Q2 FY25: ((21,803 - 21,544) / 21,544) * 100 ≈ 0.93%
- Q2 FY25 to Q1 FY25: ((21,544 - 20,417) / 20,417) * 100 ≈ 5.93%

- Q3 FY25 to Q2 FY25: ((4,798 - 4,545) / 4,545) * 100 ≈ 5.45%

For HCL Software:
- Q3 FY25 to Q2 FY25: ((3,387 - 2,865) / 2,865) * 100 ≈ 18.04%
- Q2 FY25 to Q1 FY25: ((2,865 - 3,422) / 3,422) * 100 ≈ -16.29%

Based on the growth rates, HCL Software had the highest growth rate of 18.04% from Q2 FY25 to Q3 FY25.

Question 6: What is the operating margin trend across the quarters?
Answer: To determine the operating margin trend across the quarters, we need to calculate the operating margin for each quarter. The operating margin is calculated by dividing the profit before tax by the total revenue.

From the retrieved context, we have the following information:

- Q2 FY25: Profit before tax = 5,687 crores, Total revenue = 28,862 crores
- Q3 FY25: Profit before tax = 6,132 crores, Total revenue = 29,890 crores
- Q4 FY25: Profit before tax = 6,132 crores (Note: This is the same as Q3 FY25, but we will use it for Q4 FY25 as it is the only available figure for Q4 FY25)

We can calculate the operating margin for each quarter as follows:

- Q2 FY25: Operating margin = (5,687 / 28,862) * 100 = 19.68%
- Q3 FY25: Operating margin = (6,132 / 29,890) * 100 = 20.56%
- Q4 FY25: Operating margin = (6,132 / 30,246) * 100 = 20.28%

The operating margin trend across the quarters is an increase from Q2 FY25 to Q3 FY25, and then a slight decrease in Q4 FY25.
